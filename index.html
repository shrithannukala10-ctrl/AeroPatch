The AeroPatch process

From road damage to a repair plan.

The website is the first part of the idea: collect clear reports and locations before testing any physical drone prototype.

01

People report

A road user shares the pothole location, approximate size, and a short description. A photo can be added in a future version.

02

Teams review

Reports can help a project team or authorised road-maintenance team prioritise inspection. Reports are not automatically verified.

03

Drone concept

The proposed drone lands its four support legs on stable road around the pothole, then uses a central nozzle to dispense suitable repair material.

Why AeroPatch?

Build technology around real problems.

We imagine a future where clear reporting and carefully tested robotics can help road-maintenance teams respond more effectively.

A

Safety first

People should report hazards only from a safe place. Any drone testing must be supervised and kept away from public traffic.

B

Engineering with care

Material choice, adhesion, drainage, weather resistance, payload, and stable landing need testing before any road repair claim.

C

Transparent progress

Future versions could show report status, responsible authority, and verified repair updates—without exposing reporters' private information.

✳AeroPatch
